←→ move Space jump (again mid-air: double jump) →→ dash (double tap) ↓ drop through platform · mid-air: ground pound Shift run E hack (hold) P pause M while paused / Esc in the terminal: level select

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen.

Level 01: you broke into a megacorp network. Collect data packets, crack the encrypted [A3] blocks from below (or ground pound them), and take out sentinels by stomping, dashing through them, or pounding the floor near them. Laser firewalls block the way: stand at the terminal and hold E to bring them down. Reach the ROOT mainframe.
Level 02: find the admin password hidden in the memory dump. Pick a word with the mouse, a tap, or arrows + Enter. A wrong guess tells you how many letters match in the right position. Bracket pairs like (..) or [..] on one row purge a decoy or restore attempts. Four attempts.
Level 03: a 90s-style point-and-click. Pick a verb (Look at, Pick up, Talk to), then click things in the shop. Check K.'s requirements on your phone, inspect the laptops, talk to Flux, and buy the right rig within budget.
Level 04: route power traces on a motherboard. Click a cell (or arrows + Enter) to place the next piece; clicking a placed piece swaps it. After a 45-second countdown the current flows from the PSU: get it through the CPU, both RAM sticks, the cooler and the Wi-Fi before it hits a dead end. F speeds things up.
Level 05: collect every bitcoin in the vault. Some are in the corridors, the rest are hidden in encrypted crates: drop a logic bomb with Space to blow them open. Four bots hunt you, and blasts take out bots and you alike. On touch, tap toward a direction to move, tap m0tz (or HACK) to drop a bomb.
Level 06: a top-down dungeon. Swing the EMP baton (Space), use things with E, and take out the pocket synth with Q to play notes with the arrows. Clear the sentry room for a key, solve the block puzzle, learn the melody from the terminal, and play it at the sealed door.
Level 07: ride the hoverboard up the night street. Steer with left/right, speed up or slow down with up/down, and throw data drives into the amber mailboxes of subscriber houses on both sides: Q throws left, E throws right, Space aims for you (on touch, tap left or right of m0tz). Dodge cars, trash bots, drones and steam vents, and grab drive bundles on the road. Serve at least half the subscribers.
Level 08: dig through circuit dust with the arrows and collect data cores. Dead drives fall and roll off each other: push them sideways, and don't stand under one when it drops. Spark bugs crawl along the walls; drop a drive on one to blow it up (it leaves a core). The uplink opens once you have enough cores. R restarts if you get stuck.
Level 09: a vertical shooter. Fly m0tz's stolen interceptor over the megacorp city with the arrows (on touch, drag anywhere). The guns fire on their own; X or Space sets off an EMP blast that wipes enemy bullets. Chips flash between cyan (spread gun) and magenta (piercing laser): grab one in your weapon's color to power up. Take down the BLACK ICE fortress at the uplink.
Final level: first person. Arrows (or W/S and Q/E) move and turn, A/D strafe, Space fires. On desktop, click the screen to look around with the mouse and click to fire; Esc releases it. Find the keycard, bring down the firewall, and destroy the AI core.
Bonus: a sunset run. Your battery drains constantly: grab energy cans and batteries to keep going. Crack open capsules for EMP discs (throw with X) or a hoverboard (faster, and it absorbs one hit), but watch out for the virus. Jump rocks, avoid fire and critters, and reach the beacon.
Music: original 8-bit loop on the level select, an original slow vaporwave track on Night Route, and an original lo-fi loop in the other levels.
